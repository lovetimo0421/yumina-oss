# Studio AI

The Studio's AI assistant is a dedicated AI helper built into the Yumina editor. It helps you build world content faster ╰(*°▽°*)╯

## What It Can Do

- **Create entries**: characters, lore, world rules, greeting messages
- **Create variables**: with the right types, defaults, and behavior rules set automatically
- **Create rules**: event triggers, conditions, and actions — all correctly wired
- **Build custom UI**: full TSX interfaces from a description
- **Edit existing content**: modify, rewrite, or extend anything already in your world
- **Validate your world**: check for broken references, logic loops, and common mistakes
- **Search your world**: find content across all entries, variables, and rules

## How to Use It

Open the Studio editor and describe what you want in the chat. Be as natural and casual as you like:

> "Add a cunning merchant character named Vex who speaks in riddles and has trust issues"

> "Create a hunger system that decreases over time and kills the player at zero"

> "Build a dark fantasy UI with health, mana, and an inventory grid"

> "The combat feels too easy — make enemies do more damage and add a wound system"

After Studio AI creates or modifies content, you can review and approve or reject each change before it takes effect.

## Tips for Better Results

**Build iteratively.** Work in layers. Nail the character and setting first, then add mechanics, then polish. Don't try to describe your entire world in one message.

**Let it validate.** After several rounds of changes, ask Studio AI to confirm everything still works.

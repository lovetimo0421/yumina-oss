# Playing a world

Open Discover, pick a world, press Play, and type what you do. The AI plays the narrator and every character, so there are no fixed choices.

## Finding a world

Recommended is your feed and Following is the creators you follow. The two tabs with odd names are Resource market, prompts and card modules for people who build, and Plaza, the gallery of pictures players made. Worlds in other languages stay hidden until you turn on Other languages in Filters.

## Saves

Each time you start a world you get a new save, and you can keep as many as you like for the same world. Sessions lists them. If a world exists in several languages, New Session asks which version to start.

## In the chat

On a computer, Enter sends and Shift+Enter starts a new line, and Settings > Display can swap the two. If the creator built an interface, it appears beside the chat with things like health bars, maps and inventories.

Two message actions are easy to miss. Revert to here deletes everything after that message, and Remember this pins a sentence so the AI never forgets it.

**Another version of a reply.** The arrows under a reply make a new version or go back to an earlier one. Each version keeps its own game numbers, so if a reply raised affection to 13, the next version starts again from 10. Continue extends the version you are on.

**When the model refuses.** A reply marked Looks like a refusal can be retried or sent to another model.

**Pictures.** Send image in the + menu, or paste or drop one in, when the model supports images. Draw this scene under any reply makes a picture of how it ends. Illustrate every reply in the + menu does it for every turn, in a fast style or a detailed one. Post a picture to the Plaza and other players can use its recipe.

**Voice.** The microphone turns speech into text for free. Voice readout in Settings > Display reads replies aloud and costs mushies.

**Fullscreen.** In fullscreen the top bar hides. Move your mouse to the top edge, or pull the handle down on a phone, for the way back to your library and the Model and Memory options. Settings > Display can open worlds with their own interface in fullscreen by itself.

## Keeping a character's face the same

A LoRA is a small model file that teaches the AI one character or art style, so illustrations draw that character with the same face, hair and clothes every time. The LoRA training page in the Atelier is where they live: train one from a single portrait, import one from a Civitai link, or add one from the LoRA square. Then put it on your persona so you look like yourself in pictures, or, on your own cards, on a character or the card's art style. The Vivid illustration style draws one person per picture. [More on illustration LoRAs](../creator/visuals-audio.md#illustration-loras).

## Branching

Branch from here on any message starts a new save from that point, with everything before it kept. Branches in the + menu shows the tree.

## Playing with friends

Worlds with a **Multiplayer** badge can be played together. Press **Play with friends** next to **Start Playing**: your save starts and an invite sheet opens with a link, a short code and your friends list. Friends join only by invite. If the world has roles, each friend picks one when joining.

In a shared story, everyone writes what they do each round and the AI answers once for the whole table. Card and board games run on the world's own table, where the host can seat AI players in empty seats. The host pays for the AI. On an adult world, everyone must be 18 or older with a birth year in their profile.

Live games, with a Live badge, have their own Invite friends inside the game.

## Exporting and lost worlds

Export session transcript on a world's page in your library saves a chat as a text file in your Assets. If a creator takes a world down, your saves stay readable, but you cannot send new messages.

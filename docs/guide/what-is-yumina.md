# What is Yumina

Yumina is where you play and create AI driven games, visual novels, anime characters and simulators. Creators build the worlds. You step in and play them with AI characters that talk back and remember you.

Nothing is scripted in advance. Type what you do, and the AI plays the narrator and every character around you. Kick down the door and it describes what happens. Talk your way out instead and you get a different scene. Behind the story, the game keeps numbers like health, money and relationships, and they change only when the story earns it. Creators can show them as health bars, maps, inventories and character art.

What you can do here:

- Browse worlds in every genre: romance, horror, mystery, sci-fi, survival, comedy.
- Keep several saves per world, branch a story at any message, and go back to a checkpoint.
- Pick the AI model, how creative it is, and how much it remembers.
- Play with friends in a multiplayer room.
- Rate worlds, follow creators, and tip the ones that made your week.

It runs in your browser, it is free to play, and it is built on an open-source AI game engine.

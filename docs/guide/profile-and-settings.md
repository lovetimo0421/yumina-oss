# Profile, personas and settings

Your profile shows your avatar, banner, bio, playtime, recent worlds, published worlds, favorites and achievements. Follow a creator there and their new worlds show up in your Following tab.

## Personas

A persona is the character you play as, with a name, avatar, appearance, personality and backstory, plus extra entries for things like abilities or rules. The AI reads all of it on every turn, so a scared teenager and a hardened detective get different stories from the same world. Without a persona the AI only knows your display name, and plenty of people play that way.

Create personas on your profile. One is active for all your chats, Persona for new sessions can fix a different default for new saves, and any save can lock its own choice. The Note on a persona is private and never sent to the AI. Keep a persona short and leave room for the world. A backstory that says you are the chosen one fights the story the creator wrote.

## Reviews and Discussion

Use Reviews to give a world one to five stars and optional text in the same editor. New submissions use one active review per world, including its language variants. Older posts stay in Reviews, including comments without stars and multiple posts by the same person. **Add update** lets you change the rating, add a dated update, or both. Your original text stays as written. Rating changes show the old and new star icons. Reason tags stay with the update that introduced them. One- and two-star ratings need a reason selection; written details are optional. Comments stay beneath the review.

Reviews show your current playtime and playtime when you first reviewed the world. Updates keep the original snapshot. Older reviews may show that this value is unavailable. Long text has **More** and **Collapse** controls.

Use Discussion for questions, bug reports and conversations without a rating. Start with a title and body. You can edit your own discussion or comment for three minutes after posting.

**Delete** offers **Undo** for five seconds. A post with only your own comments disappears with those comments. Posts with other people's comments leave a deleted placeholder. Comments with child replies also leave a placeholder; comments without children disappear. Deleting your active review removes its vote. Deleting an untouched historical post does not change your current vote.

## Settings that are easy to miss

- **Content & Safety.** Limited hides adult worlds. Limitless shows them, if the birth year on your account makes you an adult.
- **Privacy.** Who sees your profile, whether people can message you, and whether your play history, favorites and follower lists show. Blacklist blocks people. The [privacy page](privacy.md) explains each.
- **Play appearance.** In Settings → Display / Language → Play appearance, choose Dark or Light for the default chat. Dark is the default. Your browser remembers the choice. Worlds with custom interfaces keep their own colours.
- **Display.** Press Enter to send, and fullscreen by itself for worlds with their own interface. Voice readout costs mushies, voice input is free. Scene video turns replies into film at the price the switch shows. The interface comes in English, Chinese, Spanish and Japanese, and worlds stay in the language their creator wrote.

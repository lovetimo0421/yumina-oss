# Your library and saves

Your library is every world you played or saved. Favorites stay at the top, and you can filter by Favorites, Updated, Not played, or one of your collections.

Collections are your own groups, named however you like. A world can be in several. Press **+** beside the filters, name it and tick its worlds in the same window. You can also open a world and use **Add to collection** under Collections, or press **Select**, pick worlds and use **Add to collection**. Pick a collection and press **⋯** next to it to edit its name and worlds, or delete it; deleting a collection keeps its worlds.

**Hide from library** (in a world's **⋯** menu, or in Select) takes a world off the list without touching its saves. Hidden worlds are under **Hidden**, where **Show in library** brings them back.

Open a world to see its saves. Play continues one or starts another, and each save has its own story and numbers. Edit as My Project makes a copy you can change in Studio without touching the original, when the author allows it. Remove from Library also deletes your saves and play history for that world, and there is no undo. To keep the saves, hide it instead.

## Your projects, files and bundles

My Projects holds worlds you made or copied, split into drafts and published. Assets holds the images, audio, video and fonts you uploaded, in folders, and you can drop in a ZIP to import many at once. Storage depends on your plan. Bundles are asset packs you collected from creators.

# Supporting creators

If a world made your week, the Support button sends the creator money or mushies. It is in the chat header while you play, on the world's page, and on the creator's profile.

Money tips go through Stripe, from $1 to $500. Yumina keeps 20%, and Stripe takes its processing fee. Mushie gifts start at 50 and reach the creator the same instant, with no fee. An account that has never paid can gift up to 200 a month.

With both you can add a short message and tip anonymously, which hides your name from the creator and from the world's supporters list, one tip at a time.

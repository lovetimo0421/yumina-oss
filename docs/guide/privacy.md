# Privacy

Your chats are private. Nobody else can read them, including the creator of the world, who only sees play counts. We do not use your messages or your worlds to train AI, and we pick providers whose terms say the same.

## Your profile

Settings > Privacy sets who sees your profile: everyone, people you follow, or nobody. Separate switches cover private messages, your play history, your favorites and your follower lists, and Blacklist keeps people away from you. Tips can be anonymous, one tip at a time.

## Your AI traffic

With your own key, your messages go to the provider you chose and we never bill them. With a model on your own computer through Ollama or LM Studio, nothing leaves your machine at all. Keys are stored encrypted and never shown again once saved.

## What we keep

Deleting a save or removing a world from your library is final, so export anything you want to keep. We do not sell your data, and analytics only tell us how the product is used so we can fix it. To delete your account, open Settings > Account > Delete account and confirm the link we email you. Your data is gone within 7 days.

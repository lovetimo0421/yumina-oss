# Supporting Creators

Yumina creators put real work into their worlds. If a world made your week, you can give them something back -- either real money or in-platform **mushies**. Both flow to the creator; the platform takes a flat 20% fee on money tips and nothing on mushie gifts.

## Two ways to support

| | Money Tip | Mushie Gift |
|---|---|---|
| **Currency** | Real money via Stripe | Mushies (in-app currency) |
| **Presets** | $1 ☕ · $3 🧋 · $7 🍱 · $15 🎁 | 150 · 500 · 1,500 🍄 |
| **Custom amount** | $1 -- $500 | Min 50 mushies |
| **Goes to** | Creator's Stripe payout balance | Creator's mushie wallet |
| **Fees** | 20% platform fee + Stripe processing | None |

Both flows let you attach a short message and choose to give anonymously.

## Where to find the Support button

You'll see a heart-icon **Support** button on:

- The **chat header** while playing any session (creators of the world you're in)
- The **world preview modal** when you tap a world card in the Hub or Library
- The **library detail panel** when you open a world's full page
- Other players' **public profiles**, when they have at least one published world

You can't tip yourself -- the button is hidden on your own worlds.

## The tipping flow

1. Click **Support** anywhere it appears
2. Pick **Money** or **Mushies** at the top of the modal
3. Choose a preset or enter a custom amount
4. (Optional) Write a short message the creator will see in their notifications
5. (Optional) Toggle **Anonymous** if you don't want the creator to see your name
6. Confirm

**Money tips** use Stripe Payment Element -- you can pay with card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, or other digital wallets depending on your region. WeChat Pay is offered for Chinese users.

**Mushie gifts** deduct from your wallet immediately and land in the creator's account the same instant. Mushies you gift come from your **addon balance** (mushies you purchased or received as bonuses) -- not from your plan's daily refresh floor. If you're below the floor, the modal will show what you have available to gift.

## Anonymous vs. named

- **Named** — the creator sees your username and avatar in their notifications, in their **Recent Support** feed, and on the world's public **supporters** list.
- **Anonymous** — appears as "Anonymous" to the creator and in public lists. The creator does not see who you are; your tip still goes through normally.

The anonymous flag is per-tip, so you can be public on one world and private on another.

## After you tip

- You'll see a "Thanks!" confirmation toast.
- The creator gets an in-app notification with your name (or "Anonymous"), the amount, and your message if you wrote one.
- On the world's public page, named tips show up in the **supporters** list. Anonymous tips contribute to the world's support totals but don't list your name.

## What the creator actually receives

For money tips:
- **Platform fee:** 20% of the gross tip
- **Stripe processing:** deducted separately by Stripe
- **Hold period:** 7 days after the payment succeeds (refund/dispute window)
- **Minimum payout:** $10 -- creators can cash out once their available balance crosses this threshold
- **Payout method:** Stripe Connect Express -- creators link their own bank account through Stripe

For mushie gifts:
- 100% of the mushies you sent land in the creator's wallet immediately, with no hold.

## FAQ

**Can I refund a tip?**  
Money tips go through Stripe's standard dispute process. Mushie gifts are non-refundable once sent.

**Will my message be public?**  
No -- messages are only visible to the recipient.

**Is there a minimum tip?**  
$1 for money tips, 50 mushies for gifts.

**Can creators block tips?**  
The Support button is automatically hidden on profiles without any published worlds.

**Does Yumina take a cut of mushie gifts?**  
No. The full mushie amount transfers from your wallet to the creator's.

**Where do creators see this?**  
In their [Earnings Dashboard](/creator/earnings) -- per-world breakdowns, charts, top supporters, and a full history.

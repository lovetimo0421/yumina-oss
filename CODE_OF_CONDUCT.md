# Code of conduct

This project follows the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1. Read the full text here:

https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/

The short version:

- Be kind. Assume the other person is trying to help.
- Be patient with beginners. Everyone was one.
- Disagree about code, not about people.
- No harassment, no slurs, no personal attacks, no sexual content aimed at anyone, no publishing of
  private information. Not in issues, not in PRs, not in chat, not anywhere connected to the project.

This applies to everyone in every project space: the repository, discussions, release notes, and
any place you represent the project.

## Reporting

If someone breaks these rules, or you feel unsafe, email `support@yumina.io`. Tell us what happened,
where, and when. Screenshots or links help.

We read every report. We keep the reporter's identity private. We will answer within a week and
tell you what we did.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues, and PRs that break these rules. They may
warn, temporarily block, or permanently ban people who keep breaking them. The Contributor Covenant
describes the ladder we use.

Maintainers are held to the same rules. If a maintainer is the problem, say so in the report and a
different maintainer will handle it.
